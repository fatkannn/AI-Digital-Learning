# Learning Goals

## Primary Goal

Build practical and conceptual competence in AI and digital technology
from beginner level toward advanced applied capability.

## Core Competencies

### Digital

-   Understand computers, operating systems, networks, web applications,
    servers, databases, cloud, and security fundamentals.
-   Understand how modern digital products are architected.

### AI

-   Understand AI, machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, LLMs,
    multimodal AI, RAG, agents, embeddings, and evaluation.
-   Understand what AI can and cannot reliably do.

### Development

-   Learn programming fundamentals, especially Python and JavaScript.
-   Understand Git, APIs, JSON, databases, authentication, deployment,
    and debugging.

### Automation

-   Understand workflow automation, n8n, APIs, webhooks, agents, and
    MCP.
-   Build useful automated workflows.

### Applied AI

-   Build AI-assisted learning, e-learning, LMS, assessment, content,
    multimedia, and knowledge-management solutions.

## Learning Success Criteria

Progress should be measured by demonstrated ability, not by completion
of tutorials.

Evidence of learning includes: - Explaining a concept without copying a
definition. - Building a small working implementation. - Debugging a
failure. - Comparing alternatives. - Identifying limitations and
risks. - Making and defending a technical decision.

## Career-Relevant Direction

Favor projects and exercises that can strengthen capability in: -
AI-enabled learning technology. - E-learning development. - Digital
learning products. - AI automation. - AI-assisted development. -
Technical consulting and solution design.
