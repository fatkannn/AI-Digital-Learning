## Selected direction

**Aetheris Narrative** is the selected base direction for the AI & Digital Learning Journey.

## Why

- It best supports a calm, guided, editorial learning experience.
- It is compatible with the approved Entry Experience.
- It can scale across Introduction, Module Overview, Micro-learning, Recall, and Assessment.
- It avoids turning the product into a dense LMS or generic SaaS dashboard.

## Usage rule

Aetheris is a direction, not a pixel-perfect implementation source. Preserve the existing Entry Experience and adapt the visual language to the current application components and interaction behavior.

The other directions are retained as alternatives:

- Editorial Clarity
- Ethereal Intelligence
- Guided Editorial
- Lumina Intellect
- Lumina Editorial

Use them only for selective comparison or a specific pattern that improves comprehension and scalability.
